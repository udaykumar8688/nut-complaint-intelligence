Our project, in plain language

NUT is a smarter first desk
for customer support.

Companies receive many customer complaints every day. Sorting each one manually takes time, and sending it to the wrong department delays help. Our project explores how Natural Language Processing can make that first decision faster and more consistent.

We trained a learning model with sample complaints that were already labelled by category. When a new message arrives, NUT compares its wording with those examples and recommends the most suitable team.

Why it matters

Faster routing can help a customer reach the person who can solve their problem sooner.

Project snapshot2026
Problem

Complaint messages are unstructured and can be difficult to sort quickly.

Solution

An NLP classifier that suggests one of six queues: Billing, Customer Service, Technical Support, Account Management, Security & Fraud, or Orders & Delivery.

Value

A clearer first response path, with visible confidence instead of a hidden decision.

Responsible use

This classroom prototype uses fictional sample data. Real deployment requires approved data, human review, and privacy controls.
