# Synthetic training dataset

`complaints.csv` contains 180 fictional customer complaints created for this
project. It has 30 examples in each category: `Billing`, `Customer Service`,
`Technical Support`, `Account Management`, `Security & Fraud`, and
`Orders & Delivery`.

The data contains no real customers, personal information, or CFPB complaint
records. It is suitable for demonstrating the training and prediction workflow,
but it must be replaced or expanded with real, correctly labelled complaints
before a production deployment.
